Engineering findings

The first concept was 444 g overweight. The CAD mass check showed a 50 × 9.5 mm bar weighs 773 g and full titanium plates ~400 g each. A thinner bar with slots near the hub, a tapered plate and pocketing brought it under the limit while keeping ~940 J.
Gyroscopic lift is about 11× gravity in fast turns. Expected for horizontal spinners. The driver should turn gently at full spin.
Battery margin is thin. If testing confirms it, an 850 mAh pack fits inside the weight margin.

Motor kV, winding resistance and some part masses are datasheet estimates. Update the parameters after weighing real parts and rerun.
