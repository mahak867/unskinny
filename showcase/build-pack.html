Mk II redesign · Beetleweight 3 lb (1361 g) · horizontal bar spinner

Unskinny Bot Build Pack

A ground-up remake of lewisb42's 2013 spinner, using the layout that wins beetleweight events today: a direct-drive brushless weapon on a hub motor, brushless geared drive, 4S power and a hardened steel bar. Everything you need to order, design, build and test is here. Tick boxes as you go; they're saved in this browser.

00What changed and why

Top horizontal spinners in current 3 lb brackets (for example Caldera, a horizontal midcutter, and the many bar spinners in the NHRL 2025 Championship qualifier list) share a few traits. Mk II adopts all of them.

2013 original

Unskinny Mk I

  • Belt drive, 1600 kV outrunner, no tensioner
  • Aluminum bar, 6 in long
  • Brushed B16 drive, 3S 1000 mAh
  • Aluminum + UHMW box frame
  • Belt jumps off on big hits
Mk II

Unskinny Mk II

  • Direct-drive hub motor (Repeat 2812 class): blade bolts to the motor bell, no belt or pulleys
  • Hardened steel bar (AR500 or S7), ~9 in, ~300 g
  • Brushless planetary drive (Repeat Max V2 class)
  • 4S LiPo for more weapon and drive power
  • Thick TPU frame to absorb recoil, titanium top/bottom plates
  • Low, invertible profile with replaceable front forks

03System layout

flowchart LR
  BAT["4S LiPo 650 mAh"] --> SW["Power switch / link"]
  SW --> DESC["Dual brushless drive ESC"]
  SW --> WESC["Weapon ESC 60–80 A, 4S"]
  DESC --> ML["Left Max V2 drive"]
  DESC --> MR["Right Max V2 drive"]
  WESC --> HUB["2812 hub motor + steel bar"]
  RX["Receiver with failsafe"] -.->|"CH1/CH2 mixed"| DESC
  RX -.->|"CH3"| WESC

The weapon motor sits at the center of the chassis between the top and bottom plates, bolted through both. The battery and ESCs sit in the rear pods behind the drive motors, as far from the weapon as possible. Wheels poke out past both plates slightly so the bot drives either way up.
